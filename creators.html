FAQ

Common questions from creators.

What creators ask before they apply.

Who is Junglee Academy for?

TikTok Shop affiliate creators with 5,000+ followers who want to grow their TikTok Shop earnings. Junglee currently works on TikTok Shop only.

What does it cost?

Our fee is 20% of the TikTok Shop affiliate commission you earn through TikTok Shop's creator program, and you keep 80%. TikTok pays each side directly. For example, on a $40 product with a 20% commission ($8), you keep $6.40 and Junglee receives $1.60.

What does the 20% apply to?

Only your TikTok Shop affiliate commission earned through TikTok Shop's creator program. It does not apply to off-platform brand deals, flat fees, Creator Rewards or LIVE gifts. Those are 100% yours.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. It is month-to-month. You can end the agency link in TikTok at any time, and ending it ends your Academy access.

Can I work with other brands or agencies?

You are not exclusive to any brand, and brand deals you find are yours. We coach you on working with brands, but we do not manage your brand relationships unless we agree a separate agency-management arrangement. TikTok allows one agency per creator on TikTok Shop, so linking Junglee means Junglee is your TikTok Shop agency.

What happens after I apply?

We review applications within 1–2 business days. If approved, you accept Junglee as your TikTok Shop agency inside TikTok, then get access to Junglee Academy, your creator dashboard, matched opportunities and platform support.

I have under 5,000 followers. Can I still join?

The Academy starts at 5,000 followers, past TikTok's Creator Pilot limits. Until then, start with our free starter lesson and come back to apply when you reach 5K.

Why can Junglee be my TikTok Shop agency?

Junglee Studios is an Official TikTok Shop Full-Service Management Partner, with Creator Agency Partner (CAP), TikTok Affiliate Partner (TAP) and Multi-Channel Network (MCN) status. That status is what allows creators to link Junglee as their agency inside TikTok.

What does Junglee do that I can't do alone?

Stage-based coaching and LIVE coaching from people who helped build TikTok Shop, a creator dashboard built on TikTok's official APIs, product and sample opportunities in your creator portal as brand partners join, and help with violations, tickets and platform changes. We cannot promise specific results; what you earn depends on your content, products and consistency.
