FAQ

Common questions about the Academy.

Is Junglee Academy approved by TikTok?

Yes. Before TikTok gave us access to its official TikTok Shop APIs, we submitted the full app and a live demo for their review. Junglee Academy is also listed in the apps section of TikTok Shop Seller Center.

Is it an app I download?

No. Junglee Academy is a web app, so you log in from your browser. There is nothing to install.

Where does the data come from?

Straight from TikTok, through TikTok Shop's official APIs. Your sales, commissions and performance show up without spreadsheets or manual exports.

How do brands get access?

Brands on Junglee Managed Services get the Academy as part of the program. Self-managed plans (Junglee Academy for Brands) are coming soon. See brand services.

How do creators get access?

Creators with 5,000+ followers apply, and once approved, link Junglee as their TikTok Shop agency inside TikTok. Our fee is 20% of your TikTok Shop affiliate commission and you keep 80%. See the creator deal.

Is the Academy content protected?

Yes. All Academy content is watermarked with the member's name. Downloads are disabled, copy-paste is restricted, and printing is blocked.
