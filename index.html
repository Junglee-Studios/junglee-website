How the 12 months work

We build it with your team.
Then your team runs it.

For 12 months we operate the program alongside your team, write playbooks for your products and audience, and help you hire and train the people who will run it. At 90 days we review what is gaining traction and adjust the playbook. By the end, your team owns the channel and the affiliate program.

Results on TikTok Shop take time and depend on your category, products, inventory, campaigns, and team bandwidth. We don't promise growth targets.
