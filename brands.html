FAQ

Common questions from brands.

Everything we get asked on the first call. If yours isn't here, it's a good first question on the strategy call.

How much does it cost?

Junglee Managed Services is $7,500/mo plus 5% of shop GMV on a 12-month program, plus a performance kicker: a premium rate that applies only to GMV above your trailing 90-day baseline, so we earn it only on growth we help create. Content production and LIVE shows are add-ons, priced separately: Branded Live Selling w/Host Program is $3,500/mo + 5% of LIVE GMV, LIVE Specialist Support is $3,500/mo + 7% commission, and Content Creation is custom-quoted. Standalone Affiliate Management is $4,000/mo + 5% of affiliate GMV.

How long does it take to see results?

Growth on TikTok Shop is real but it takes time, and the pace depends on your category, products, inventory, campaigns, and how much your team can collaborate. Not every brand sells the same way — some lean into LIVE or auctions, others grow through affiliates and video. That is why we build a playbook around your brand instead of promising a standard growth curve — and why Junglee Managed Services is a 12-month program: lasting results on TikTok Shop come from compounding work over time, not a 90-day sprint. At day 90 we review with your leadership what is gaining traction and adjust the playbook, and you see weekly reporting the whole way.

We don't have a TikTok Shop yet. Can you help us launch?

Yes. Launches are a core part of Junglee Managed Services: shop setup, catalog and listing optimization, your first affiliate campaigns, and content and LIVE playbooks for your team. Starting right avoids the policy issues, weak listings, and wasted samples that slow most new shops down.

Can we hire you for just one part, like affiliates or LIVE?

Yes. Affiliate Management, Branded Live Selling w/Host Program, LIVE Specialist Support, and Content Creation are each available as standalone services for brands already running their shop.

Who is Junglee built for?

Established brands ready to treat TikTok Shop as a real revenue channel, not a side experiment. Typically D2C-native or D2C-ready, with product-market fit, inventory capacity, and the willingness to lean in aggressively. Smaller brands with the capability and ambition to invest meaningfully are absolutely a fit — what matters is readiness to scale, not a fixed revenue floor.

What does working with Junglee actually look like?

We work alongside your team for a full 12-month program, building playbooks tailored to your brand and running the strategy and operations together — shop operations, affiliate campaigns, promotions, and ads, plus content and LIVE playbooks your team executes. We don't run a black-box service; we share what we're doing, how, and why, so your team can own the program. By the end, you have a documented operating model and a team that knows how to run it.

What does the first 90 days look like?

It depends on where you start. If you are launching, the first 90 days are about building the foundation: shop setup, listings, policy and fulfillment readiness, AI customer service, and your first affiliate campaigns, with content and LIVE playbooks for your team. If you are already selling — say $50K a month — we start with an audit of what is driving and limiting sales, then focus on scaling what already works: affiliate campaigns, promotions, and the content and LIVE formats converting for you. Either way, at day 90 we review with your leadership what is gaining traction and adjust the plan for the rest of the year.

What happens at the end of the 12-month engagement?

Your team owns it. The whole program is designed around handing over a working channel, not building a permanent dependency: playbooks, systems, and an affiliate program that belongs to your brand. Some brands keep us on an advisory retainer or for add-on services, but the core operations should be running inside your team.

What certifications does Junglee hold?

Junglee Studios is a TikTok Shop Certified Full-Service Management Partner — the badge you see on every page of this site. Junglee was founded by Jatin "JT" Patel, who served on the founding team at TikTok Shop's U.S. launch, where he co-led the build of the Collectibles category from zero and led Countdown Bidding from concept to live. The team brings insider knowledge of how the platform actually rewards brands and creators, not third-party guesswork.

How does Junglee work with creators on behalf of our brand?

Junglee Managed Services includes full affiliate management, built specifically for your brand and products — not a generic creator list. Here's what that covers:

  • Program design. Commission structure, open and targeted plans, and which products to lead with — set by your margins, price points, and category.
  • Creator research. We find creators already selling similar products or in your category, and vet them on real sales performance, audience fit, and posting consistency — not follower counts.
  • Outreach & collaborations. Targeted collaborations, invitations, and a creator brief for each product: the hooks, talking points, and proof that make your product sell on video and LIVE.
  • Sample strategy. Samples go to creators likely to post and convert, and we track every one until it turns into content — so samples are an investment, not a giveaway.
  • Campaign activation. Commission boosts, promotions, and TikTok campaign moments that turn sampled creators into a steady stream of sales.
  • Weekly optimization. We double down on the creators and content driving sales, re-engage those who've gone quiet, and retire what isn't working — reported to you weekly.

Everything is built inside your TikTok Shop and stays tied to your brand: your creators, your relationships, your data. We don't own your affiliate program — we build it for you, document how it runs, and train your team so they can take it over once it's scaled.

Do you only do the 12-month build program, or can you just run our TikTok Shop ongoing?

Both. The 12-month program is designed for brands who want to end up owning TikTok Shop in-house — we run it with you while transferring ownership. If you'd rather keep a long-term partner, we can continue running shop operations, affiliates, campaigns, and ads after the first year, with monthly reporting. We'll confirm what fits on the first call.

What reporting and insights do we get?

Every brand engagement includes monthly performance reporting covering GMV, affiliate channel metrics, content benchmarks, LIVE performance, and growth signals. We don't just run the program and hand you a dashboard — we interpret what's happening, where the growth levers are, and what we're doing about them. Brands on Junglee Managed Services get real-time visibility into affiliate campaigns, creator performance, and shop ops throughout the month.
